Assignment: AI-Powered Frontend Website Cloning
Agent
Objective
Build an AI Agent that takes a publicly accessible website URL and automatically
recreates its frontend/UI.
The goal is to evaluate your ability to build an AI-powered engineering system that
can analyze an unfamiliar website, generate a frontend, and modify it using
natural-language instructions.

the original website.
